# CC-029 — Stables integration is dead — affiliate config and Module 4 links

`src/lib/config.ts:52` declares `NEXT_PUBLIC_STABLES_AFFILIATE_URL`, which nothing in `src`
reads. `src/learn/module-4-spending-your-usdc-in-australia.md` links to `stables.money`.

Per the archived Linear notes (NOR-292 / NOR-304) Stables is sunsetting, and the MVP
definition of done marks the affiliate item superseded. Module 4 is the page that answers
"how do I actually spend this in Australia" — the most important practical question a new
Australian worker has — so a dead recommendation there is worse than no recommendation.

Fix: remove the dead config, and rewrite Module 4 around a current AUD off-ramp. Needs
research on what is actually available in Australia now.

## Update 2026-07-25 — merged from the Linear export, and the AU answer is now clear

Promoted to P1. This was two Linear issues, and the research question in the second one has
since been settled by events.

### NOR-292 — remove the affiliate references
- Audit all six `/learn` modules
- Remove `NEXT_PUBLIC_STABLES_AFFILIATE_URL` from the Vercel env **and** from the Zod schema at
  `src/lib/config.ts:52`
- `grep -ri stables src/ docs/ README.md` to confirm clean
- Stables is sunsetting, likely for AU regulatory reasons. Not an architectural dependency.

### NOR-304 — replace it with a real AU offramp
The recommendation is now easy: **Coinbase Australia**. It was granted an AFSL with retail
derivatives authorisation by ASIC in early April 2026 — the first crypto exchange to receive one
directly from the regulator — days after the Corporations Amendment (Digital Assets Framework)
Bill 2025 passed both houses on 1 April 2026.

That makes it the strongest AU recommendation available, and it has the useful property of being
the same rails the rest of the stack already sits on: Base, Smart Wallet, x402. Module 4 becomes
a much simpler story.

Decide separately whether to pursue an affiliate angle at all, or just recommend plainly. Given
the trust-first positioning of the whole project, plainly is probably better.

See CC-051 for what that same legislation means for the platform itself.

## Update 2026-08-11 — backlog triage pass

Raised P1 → **P0**. Re-read against the tree during the triage pass, and the problem is
materially worse than this issue described. Module 4 is not merely *linking* to Stables — it is
built around it: a "The Stables way: done in minutes" section, a five-step "Setting up Stables"
walkthrough, and line 55 states *"When you sign up through our link, you're supporting Carbon
Contractors directly — we're a Stables affiliate partner."*

Nothing reads `NEXT_PUBLIC_STABLES_AFFILIATE_URL` (`src/lib/config.ts:51`), so there is no live
affiliate link behind "our link". The site therefore asserts a commercial relationship that may not
exist, with a sunsetting provider, on the one page answering the most practical question an
Australian worker has. For a project whose entire strategy is demonstrated honesty that is a sharper
problem than a dead link, and it is accuracy-sensitive under Australian Consumer Law — noted as an
observation from a code review, not as legal advice.

`src/learn/module-6-dont-get-rekt.md:52` also tells workers to secure "your Stables account".

The 2026-08-10 Learn sweep rewrote Module 5, not Module 4, which is why this survived that pass.
