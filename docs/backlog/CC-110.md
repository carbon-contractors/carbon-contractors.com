# CC-110 — Diarised DAP review — lawyer brief and pre-commencement checkpoint (from CC-051)

## Why this exists

`CC-051` classified the platform against the **Corporations Amendment (Digital Assets
Framework) Bill 2025** and closed: the architecture-based "not a DAP" position is
recorded, the two exemption limbs are instrumented (`CC-085`'s
`verify-concurrent-escrow`, hourly), and `/terms` states the position publicly.

One action in `CC-051`'s list deliberately outlives the ticket: the position is an
engineering self-assessment, not legal advice, and the framework's obligations bite on
**9 April 2027** with a six-month transition after commencement. This ticket is the
diary entry, with a due date, so the runway does not silently expire.

Sequencing (carried over from `CC-051`): the ASIC/DAP question here and the AUSTRAC
question in `CC-098` are **different regimes, different regulators, different clocks**.
`CC-098` is already a hard pre-mainnet gate (`CC-039`), and the sensible move is one
lawyer engagement covering both — this ticket supplies the DAP half of the brief, and
resolves when the ASIC half is answered alongside `CC-098`'s AUSTRAC half.

## The brief, already prepared

Hand the lawyer both halves, not one:

- **The contract half** — `CC-037`'s verified record: no destination other than
  `task.worker` or a refund to `task.agent` is reachable by anyone, owner included
  (`CarbonEscrow.sol:133,171,191`, both addresses fixed on-chain at funding).
- **The oracle half** — `ADR-0001` D9: the platform is the verdict oracle in v1. That
  authority is bounded and falsifiable (published rules, published inputs, re-runnable
  result) rather than eliminated; v2 makes verdicts permissionless against a bond.
  Being unable to *copy* the HSM key while being able to *direct it to sign* is still
  control over disposition — the argument that works is "the contract will not let me
  reach the money", not "I cannot extract the key" (NOR-196).
- **The threshold context** — `ADR-0002` D8: turnover not profit ($3M gross income,
  whole entity, prior FY); escrowed USDC arguably not platform income at all, only a
  platform fee would be (accountant question); **do not structure for the exemption**.
- The question: does `CarbonEscrow` + the verdict-signing role constitute a
  **Digital Asset Platform** under the Bill — and if the answer is "arguably yes",
  what does enrolment require and when?

If the answer is "no", get the reasoning in writing so it survives being asked again
(the `CC-098` discipline). If "yes", `CC-039`'s mainnet gate is the wrong side of the
commencement date to find out.
