# CC-028 — Security audit document is published in the public repo root

`AUDIT-2026-03-25.md` (453 lines, 10 findings with file-level detail) sits in the root of a
public repository alongside a live site.

Every finding AUD-001 to AUD-008 appears to be closed on master, so the exposure is mostly
historical — but AUD-009 and AUD-010 are still open by design, and the document is a map of
where to look.

Fix: confirm every finding is closed, then either move it to a private location or trim it to
a summary. Same question for `docs/HSM-Deployer-Checklist.md`, though that one is arguably a
trust asset worth publishing.

---

## Closed 2026-07-25 — wontfix, by design

Publishing `AUDIT-2026-03-25.md` in the repo root was a deliberate transparency decision, not an
oversight, and the same reasoning applies to `docs/HSM-Deployer-Checklist.md` and
`docs/Security-Trust-Disclosure.md`.

For an unknown solo developer building an escrow service, a published audit with its findings
closed is evidence. Withdrawing it would remove the strongest available proof that the codebase has
been examined at all. All of AUD-001 to AUD-008 are closed; AUD-009 and AUD-010 are open by
documented decision.

See `CC-056` for the full reasoning and the resulting disclosure policy, and
`docs/Lessons-Learned.md` §8.
