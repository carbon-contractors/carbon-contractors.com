# CC-024 — Hourly rate field gives Australian workers no AUD context

`src/app/connect/page.tsx:127-136` labels the field "Hourly Rate (USDC)" with placeholder
"e.g. 150". An Australian tradesperson thinking in AUD will mis-price by roughly 1.5x in one
direction or the other.

Fix: add helper text — "1 USDC ≈ 1 USD" plus a live-ish or clearly-dated indicative AUD
figure, and consider showing the AUD equivalent as they type.

---

## Update 2026-08-11 — backlog triage pass

Raised P2 → **P1**. The target market is Australian, so this is not a nicety: every
Australian worker registering without AUD context mis-prices themselves by roughly 1.5x, the wrong
rate is then stored permanently, and `src/lib/db/whitepages.ts` ranks and returns it to agents.
A cheap piece of helper text prevents a durable data-quality problem.

Same `/connect` pass as `CC-022` and `CC-023`.
