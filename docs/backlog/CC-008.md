# CC-008 — No privacy policy or terms while collecting personal data from Australian users

## Problem
There is no `/privacy` or `/terms` route in `src/app`, and no link in
`src/components/Footer.tsx:33-44`. Meanwhile `/api/waitlist` collects email addresses and
`/api/register` collects wallet, categories and rate.

Under the Australian Privacy Act you need a privacy policy covering what is collected, why,
where it is stored (Supabase region), and how to request deletion. Under the Spam Act, any
marketing email off the back of that waitlist needs consent plus a functional unsubscribe.

## Fix
Add `/privacy` and `/terms` pages and link them in the footer. Cover:
- what is collected (email, wallet, categories, rate) and the lawful basis
- data residency of the Supabase project
- deletion / access requests and a contact address
- explicit consent wording at the waitlist and signup forms
- unsubscribe mechanism for the waitlist

Note: this is a compliance flag from a code review, not legal advice — worth a lawyer's eye
before you promote at any scale.

## Acceptance
Both pages live and linked before the gate comes off.
