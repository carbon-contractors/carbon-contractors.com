# CC-117 — Learn Module 4 covers only the exchange cash-out route, not card-based USDC spending

## Problem

NOR-343: Module 4 ("Spending Your USDC in Australia") presents
exchange → sell → withdraw as the only route from USDC to AUD. Since
the module was written, Mastercard-network crypto cards exist that
spend crypto balances directly at checkout — including self-custody
cards that connect to a worker's own wallet over Base (the network
Carbon Contractors pays on). The module is factually incomplete.

## Fix

Module 4 restructured around two honest routes:

- **Route 1 — the card route:** exchange-balance cards available in
  Australia today (Coinbase's AU card lineup spends against account
  balances including USDC), and self-custody cards (MetaMask Card over
  Base) presented with their verified limitation: **not AU-eligible as
  of 2026-09-24** (US/UK/EU/CAN/LatAm; even US sign-ups paused at time
  of check), framed as a watch-item with the wallet's Card tab named
  as source of truth.
- **Route 2 — the cash-out route:** the existing Coinbase Australia
  AFSL content, unchanged in substance.

Additional honest framing: checkout-time conversion means AUD/USD rate
risk between payday and shopping trip; spending on a card is generally
a taxable disposal for AU purposes (ATO guidance pointer, not advice);
DYOR section explicitly covers card availability and fees. Read time
3→4 min; hub description updated to match.

The module's standing discipline — "we name only providers we've
verified; this page can go stale" — is preserved and strengthened.

## Acceptance

- [x] Module 4 presents both routes with the custody trade-off stated.
- [x] No availability claim is made that wasn't verifiable on 2026-09-24;
  AU-unavailability of self-custody cards is stated plainly.
- [x] Tax/DYOR guidance extends to card spending, not just exchange
  cash-out.
