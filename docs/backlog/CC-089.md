# CC-089 — Remove the waitlist

Specified by `ADR-0002`, handover step 9, confirmed 2026-08-13 as the intended action at launch.

## This is a prerequisite, not tidying

`ADR-0002` D2 has the platform publish: *"the platform holds a wallet address, service categories, a
rate, and derived reputation. It does not hold, request, or verify names, emails, phone numbers,
documents or location."*

**The `waitlist` table is the only place the platform holds email addresses.** Until it is gone that
statement is false. The copy change (`CC-027`, and `/learn` Module 7) and this drop **ship
together**, or the published claim is wrong on day one.

Recorded as its own ticket specifically so it is not lost in launch-day noise.

## Scope

- Drop the `waitlist` table (migration).
- Remove the capture form from `src/components/ComingSoon.tsx`.
- Remove `src/app/api/waitlist/route.ts`, the unsubscribe page and form
  (`src/app/unsubscribe/`), and `src/lib/__tests__/waitlist-unsubscribe.test.ts`.
- Remove the `waitlist` type from `src/lib/db/types.ts`.
- Update `src/legal/privacy.md`, which currently describes waitlist collection.

## Note on notification_channels

This does **not** remove every email the platform holds. `notification_channels.address` stores an
optional contact email per worker, collected at `/connect`, and that is deliberate — a worker cannot
be told they have been hired otherwise (`CC-005`, `CC-073`).

So the `ADR-0002` D2 statement must be worded to cover it: no email is *required*, none is
verified, and any channel a worker supplies exists solely to notify them. Getting this wording
wrong is how the claim becomes false again — check it against `notification_channels` before
publishing, not after.

## Makes other issues moot

- **`CC-015`** (waitlist honeypot/captcha) — nothing to protect once the form is gone. Close as
  `wontfix` when this lands.
- **`CC-009`** (waitlist emails in logs) — the waitlist half disappears. The task-payload half
  survives; see `CC-087`.
- **`CC-016`** already closed `wontfix`.

## Acceptance

No `waitlist` table, no capture form, no waitlist route. `CC-027`'s copy claim is true when
published. Privacy policy updated.
