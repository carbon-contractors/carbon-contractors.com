# CC-091 — Succession and disaster recovery

`docs/Key-Compromise-Recovery.md` covers the key being in the **wrong** hands. This ticket covers the key
being in **nobody's** hands — the founder being deceased, incapacitated, or permanently unreachable
(the "QuadrigaCX archetype").

## Architectural Resolution (`ADR-0006`)

This design investigation graduated to **`ADR-0006` (Continuity, succession, and the right to fork)** on
2026-08-19. The full operational procedures are consolidated in **`docs/runbooks/DISASTER-RECOVERY-AND-SUCCESSION.md`**.

## Actioned Deliverables

1. **Unified Operational Runbook:** Created `docs/runbooks/DISASTER-RECOVERY-AND-SUCCESSION.md` covering end-to-end procedures for funds recovery, frontend deployment, database restore (Postgres/Supabase), Cloud KMS rotation, and discoverability repointing.
2. **Licensing Framework (`ADR-0006` D1):** AGPL-3.0-or-later declared for platform codebase with `contracts/` staying MIT.
3. **Arbitration Timeout Contract Scope (`ADR-0006` D3):** Bytecode requirements added to `CC-034` (Mainnet deploy) so arbitration timeouts default to worker claims upon platform silence.
4. **Signer / Owner Separation Architecture (`CC-090` / `ADR-0006` D2):** Decoupled operational Cloud KMS HSM signer (`kms-signer-svc`) from 2-of-3 Multisig Owner.

## Residual Single Point of Failure

Per `ADR-0006` D10: Until Gates 1 and 2 are formally executed, the authority to invoke `resolveDispute` and execute DNS failover resides solely with Aaron James Clifft. In-flight tasks settle safely without platform action via pull-payment timeouts, but manual dispute adjudication halts if the founder becomes unreachable before multisig deployment.
