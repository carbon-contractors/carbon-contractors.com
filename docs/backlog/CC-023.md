# CC-023 — Clock-skew rejection gives the user no way to self-diagnose

`src/app/api/register/route.ts:87-94` enforces a 300-second window against a timestamp taken
from the client's clock (`src/app/connect/page.tsx:50`). A phone with a skewed clock gets
"Message expired or clock skew" and no idea what to do.

Fix: return a message that names the cause and the remedy — check your device date and time
settings, enable automatic time.

---

## Update 2026-08-11 — backlog triage pass

Raised P2 → **P1**. A worker who hits this cannot register at all and is given no
diagnosable cause — a silent, unrecoverable dead end on the signup path, which is worse than its
low expected frequency suggests.

Same `/connect` pass as `CC-022` and `CC-024`.

## Resolution

Done 2026-08-21 on `feat/cc-022-023-024-connect-registration-polish`.

`src/app/api/register/route.ts` now returns, on an out-of-window timestamp:

> "Device clock is out of sync with the server. Please check your device date/time settings and
> enable automatic network time."

plus a `detail` giving the measured offset and direction (e.g. "timestamped 400s behind the
server; the allowed window is 300s") — `/connect` already renders `error: detail`, so the worker
sees both the remedy and how far out their clock is. Direction covers both failure modes
(timestamp ahead of or behind the window). Covered by new `register-route.test.ts` cases.
