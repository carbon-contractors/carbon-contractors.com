# CC-050 — Confirm Supabase connection pooling was actually addressed

NOR-180 (Supabase connection exhaustion under agent load) was **cancelled with no stated
reason.** The export flags this as worth checking rather than trusting.

The original concern stands on its face: no pooling configured, and Vercel serverless spawns
a fresh connection per invocation. Under agent load that exhausts the Postgres connection
limit, and on the free tier that limit is low.

## Do
Check whether the Supabase connection string in use is the pooler (pgbouncer, port 6543)
or a direct connection (5432), and confirm the intent. If it was genuinely deferred rather
than solved, reopen with a real decision. This is cheap to check and expensive to discover
during a launch spike.
