# CC-078 — Sepolia lifecycle: Settlement stage

> **Rescoped 2026-08-28 — read the final Update first.** Every section between here and it was
> written against the pre-`CC-082` architecture and describes a flow that no longer exists. It is
> kept for the reasoning, not as instructions. **Do not execute the steps above the rescope.**

Split out of `CC-032` in the 2026-08-11 triage pass. `CC-032` covers Discovery, `CC-077` Funding,
this covers Settlement, `CC-079` Dispute.

## Blocked

**Filed `blocked` behind `CC-080`.** This stage cannot be executed at all today:
`confirm_task_completion` calls `completeTask` as the platform signer, but the contract requires
`msg.sender == task.agent`, so it reverts unconditionally. That is not a bug this test would
discover — it was found by tracing the call path during triage, and it needs fixing before there is
anything here to test.

Do not attempt this stage until `CC-080` is done. It will fail for a known reason and prove nothing.

## Scope

The path from "the work is finished" to "the worker has the money":

deliverable submitted → `confirm_task_completion` → `completeTask()` on-chain → USDC lands in the
worker's wallet → DB flips to `completed`.

## Happy path

1. The worker submits a deliverable and `confirm_task_completion` records it.
2. The agent settles by calling `completeTask(taskId)` from its own wallet — per `CC-080`'s fix, the
   platform does not and cannot do this.
3. USDC arrives in the worker's wallet, in the exact escrowed amount.
4. **The payout destination came from on-chain escrow state, never from Supabase.** This is the
   load-bearing invariant for both fund safety and the `CC-051` regulatory position — see `CC-037`
   item 2.
5. The DB flips to `completed` **only after** the transaction succeeds, and ideally on observing the
   `TaskCompleted` event rather than on the tool call returning.
6. Measure funded → USDC received. Target: under 30 seconds for the on-chain portion.

## Unhappy paths

- Double completion reverts rather than paying twice — `task.state != Funded` gives
  `InvalidState`. This is the failure `CC-046`'s idempotency work exists to prevent at the MCP
  layer, so test both layers.
- An unauthorised caller is rejected. Note this is now the *expected* behaviour that `CC-080`
  exposed: anyone who is not `task.agent` must fail, including the platform signer.
- Over-release reverts.
- An invalid or unknown task id reverts cleanly.
- Completing a task that has already expired.

## Edge cases

- **The worker changes their wallet address in Supabase after funding.** The payout must follow the
  on-chain `task.worker` recorded at funding time, not the new DB value. This is the single most
  important edge case here — it is the direct test of the invariant in `CC-037` item 2 and of the
  `CLAUDE.md` rule that the DB is not the authority on money.
- On-chain failure mid-flight leaves the DB untouched.
- Concurrent completions do not collide on signer nonces — less pressing once settlement is
  agent-signed, but still relevant for `resolveDispute`/`expireTask`, which remain platform-signed.

## Acceptance

A real task settles end to end on Base Sepolia: an agent-held wallet calls `completeTask`, the
worker receives the exact escrowed USDC, the DB reflects `completed` only after the on-chain
confirmation, and the wallet-change edge case demonstrably pays the on-chain address rather than the
DB one. Every unhappy path above has been attempted.

## Rescoped 2026-08-28 — unblocked; settlement is a worker pull, not a platform push

**The stated blocker is gone.** This ticket says *"do not attempt until `CC-080` is done"* —
`CC-080` was **done on 2026-08-21** (PR #114). It has been executable for a week and the board did
not know.

`status: blocked` → `todo`.

### What `CC-080` and `ADR-0001` Amendment 1 changed

`completeTaskOnChain` was not fixed, it was **removed**. `completeTask` is agent-only and the
platform signer was structurally the wrong sender, so there is no platform transaction anywhere in
settlement. `confirm_task_completion` now records the confirmation and hands settlement back.

**Three routes into `Completed`**, and the worker drives two of them:

| Route | Who | When |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `releaseAfterReview` | **worker** | review window elapsed, no failing verdict presented |
| `claimWithVerdict` | **worker** | any time, presenting a passing signed verdict |
| `completeTask` | **agent** | any time — an agent may always choose to pay early |

### Happy path

1. The worker submits: `escrow.submitWork(taskId, evidenceHash, specVersionAck, attestationUid)`
   from their own wallet via the dashboard. `specVersionAck` must equal the committed `specHash` or
   it reverts `SpecAckMismatch` — that is what makes goalpost-moving impossible rather than merely
   detectable.
2. State becomes `Delivered` and `submittedAt` starts the review clock.
3. Settle by one of the three routes above. **Test all three**, and note the worker pays their own
   gas on two of them.
4. USDC arrives in the worker's wallet, exact escrowed amount.
5. **The payout destination came from on-chain state, never Supabase.** The load-bearing invariant
   (`CC-037` item 2, `CC-051`).
6. The DB flips to `completed` only after the transaction succeeds.
7. Measure funded → received for the on-chain portion.

### Unhappy paths

- Double completion reverts `InvalidState`. Test at both the contract and MCP layers (`CC-046`).
- `releaseAfterReview` **before** the window closes reverts `ReviewWindowOpen`.
- `submitWork` after the delivery deadline reverts `DeadlinePassed` — a late submission must not be
  able to force an agent through review a second after the clock they already missed.
- `submitWork` by anyone but the worker reverts `NotWorker`; `completeTask` by anyone but the agent
  reverts `NotAgent` — **including the platform signer.** That is `CC-080`'s point, now expected
  behaviour.
- `expireTask` is unreachable once `Delivered`. Confirm it reverts rather than silently refunding a
  worker who has delivered — this is the v1 defect `ADR-0001` D1 exists to fix.
- `claimWithVerdict` with a **failing** verdict reverts `VerdictResultMismatch`.
- A verdict whose `specHash`/`evidenceHash` do not bind this task reverts
  `VerdictCommitmentMismatch`; an expired one reverts; a replayed nonce reverts.

### Edge cases

- **The worker changes their wallet in Supabase after funding.** The payout must follow the on-chain
  `task.worker` fixed at funding. Still the single most important case here.
- On-chain failure mid-flight leaves the DB untouched.
- Review window at both bounds — 12h and 14d.

### Acceptance

- [ ] `submitWork` executed from the worker's own wallet, evidence hash committed.
- [ ] **All three settlement routes** exercised on Base Sepolia.
- [ ] USDC lands in the on-chain `task.worker`, never a Supabase-sourced address.
- [ ] The wallet-change edge case proves the DB is not the authority on money.
- [ ] The platform signer is demonstrably unable to settle.
- [ ] `verify-escrow-solvency.mjs` reconciles after every route.
