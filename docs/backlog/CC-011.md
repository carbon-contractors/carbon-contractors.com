# CC-011 — Stake panel demands 20 USDC from workers who have just signed up

## Problem
`src/app/dashboard/page.tsx:343-421` renders the staking panel whenever
`NEXT_PUBLIC_REPUTATION_STAKE_CONTRACT` is set, and immediately asks for a minimum 20 USDC
stake. `handleStake` (lines 205-233) needs a funded wallet and two on-chain transactions
(approve, then stake).

For a non-crypto worker on day one this is a wall: they have no USDC, no gas, and no reason
yet to trust the platform with money.

## Fix
Collapse the panel behind a short explainer for workers with zero stake, or gate it until
they have completed a first task. Make clear staking is optional and what it buys them.

Related: `CC-072` — whether the underlying stake/unstake mechanism actually works at all is
tracked separately; this issue is about the UX/content problem regardless of that outcome.

## Done 2026-08-21 — fixed on `feat/cc-010-011-017-dashboard-onboarding-and-seed`

`src/app/dashboard/page.tsx` + `dashboard.module.css`:

- The staking panel is **collapsed by default for workers with 0 stake**. It renders as a slim card
  — title with an "optional" tag and an "Add a stake" toggle — instead of an amount input demanding
  20 USDC on day one. Workers who already hold a stake get the expanded panel by default
  ("Manage stake" / "Hide" toggle); the open/closed default tracks the loaded stake amount until
  the user toggles, so it does not flip when the fetch resolves.
- Explainer copy is always visible on the card: "Optional: Boost search rank and credibility with
  a stake deposit. Staking is not required to receive jobs."

Purely presentational — `handleStake`/`handleUnstake` and the min-20 validation are untouched, and
`CC-072` (does the stake mechanism work at all) remains open and separate.
