# CC-007 — Site promises real USDC earnings while running on Sepolia testnet

## Problem
`NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_NETWORK=testnet` with Sepolia USDC addresses.

`src/components/HomePage.tsx:15-19` says "Get paid in USDC for real work" and `/connect`
asks for an hourly rate in USDC, with no indication anywhere that this is testnet.
The only disclosure is buried in `src/app/mcp-info/page.tsx:150`.

Promoting this as-is means telling people they will be paid, then paying them in worthless
test tokens. That is a reputational and arguably a consumer-law problem, not just a UX one.

## Fix — pick one
**(a) Stay on testnet:** prominent persistent banner in `PageShell`, plus explicit copy on
`/connect` framing this as an early-access pilot with test funds. Honest and shippable today.

**(b) Go to mainnet:** blocked by CC-034 and the MVP definition-of-done gate. Do not shortcut it.

Decide this before the gate comes off — it drives the whole launch narrative.

## Update 2026-08-11 — backlog triage pass

Status `todo` → **`blocked`**, behind `CC-034`. Priority stays P0.

**The premise of this issue dissolves under the confirmed launch sequence.** Aaron's decision during
the triage pass: build and test on Sepolia (`CC-076`) → migrate to mainnet and re-test with real
personal funds (`CC-039`) → *then* lift the coming-soon gate (`CC-014`). The public's first look
at the site is therefore on **mainnet**. No public visitor ever sees "Get paid in USDC for real work"
while the escrow is on Sepolia, because the gate does not lift until after the migration.

So option (a) — the persistent testnet banner and early-access-pilot framing — should **not** be
built. Writing testnet-pilot copy now creates work that must then be unwritten before launch.
**Expect this issue to close as `wontfix` once `CC-034` lands**, with the resolution being "made
moot by sequencing" rather than "fixed".

Verified while triaging, so the record is accurate if the sequence ever changes: the copy is entirely
unchanged. `src/components/HomePage.tsx:16` still reads "Get paid in USDC for real work", the
value-prop card still asserts "One USDC equals one USD", `PageShell` carries no banner of any kind,
and `src/app/mcp-info/page.tsx` remains the only page component in `src/` that mentions testnet or
Sepolia at all. If mainnet slips or the sequence is reordered, this becomes live again immediately —
hence `blocked` rather than `wontfix` today.
